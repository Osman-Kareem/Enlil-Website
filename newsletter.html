What you get

The week's decisions and data, in five minutes

Most of the decisions that shape Iraq's energy, water and climate policy are announced in Arabic, on ministry pages and official channels. Each week Enlil Center reads them for you and explains what changed.

  • This week in 60 secondsFive lines at the top of every issue, so you have the week before you scroll.
  • From the official recordCabinet decisions, ministry statements and official data, with a link to the source on every item.
  • Why it mattersOne line of analysis per item from Enlil's researchers, on energy, water, climate and the economic policy around them.
  • New on the Data HubFresh official series from the Enlil Iraq Data Hub, free to download.
