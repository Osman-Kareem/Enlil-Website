Iraq research by topic

Enlil Center is an independent research center in Baghdad. Our work on Iraq is organised around five pillars; each page below collects the reports, articles and datasets on that topic.

ClimateIraq's climate risks, national climate policy (NDC, NAP), adaptation, loss and damage, climate finance and the country's role in the UNFCCC process. WaterThe Tigris and Euphrates, water scarcity and salinity, the southern marshes, transboundary allocation, irrigation and water governance across Iraq. EnergyElectricity supply and demand, gas flaring and capture, oil markets and export resilience, renewable energy and the economics of Iraq's power sector. EconomyOil dependence, public finance, inflation and growth, private-sector development, employment and the structural reforms Iraq's economy needs. GovernanceHow Iraqi institutions make and implement policy: environmental law, inter-ministerial coordination, data and evidence in government, and local governance.
ENLIL CENTER
