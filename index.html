About Enlil Center

An independent think tank, built in Baghdad

The Enlil Center for Environment and Sustainable Development is a non-profit, non-partisan research institution. We started with climate and environment; today our work spans the five pillars that decide whether Iraq's next decade is resilient or reactive.

We conduct applied research, publish policy analysis, maintain an open data hub, and convene Iraqi institutions, civil society and international partners around evidence. Our aim is simple: that the people shaping Iraq's future — in ministries, parliament, universities, businesses and newsrooms — can reach for Iraqi evidence first.

We are named for Enlil, the Mesopotamian god of wind and storms, whose temple stood at Nippur in what is now Iraq — a reminder that this land has weathered change for six thousand years.

"Bridging rigorous research with practical, locally anchored solutions for a more resilient, prosperous Iraq."

  • Independent and non-partisanWe publish what the evidence supports, and say where it is thin.
  • Open by defaultOur data is downloadable and every figure carries its source.
  • Rooted in IraqResearch designed with, and for, the institutions that will use it.
